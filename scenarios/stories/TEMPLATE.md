# Scenario story

Copy this file (for example to `my-story.md`), fill it in, then run
`npm run draft-scenario -- scenarios/stories/my-story.md`.

**Before you write anything:** remove or change every real client name, person's
name, product codename and figure that could identify an engagement. This text
is sent to Gemini to draft the scenario.

## What happened?
What was the project, and what went wrong or got hard? When in the project did it happen?

## Who was involved?
For each person: their role, what they cared about, what they were worried about but
didn't say, and how they came across in the room.

## What made it hard?
Think about the pressure, the competing priorities, what you didn't know at the time,
and anything that changed partway through.

## What did good handling look like?
Looking back, what worked? What would you do differently?

## How hard should this be? (1–5)
★ gentle · ★★ one serious problem · ★★★ a twist mid-conversation · ★★★★ crisis · ★★★★★ brutal

## Should it reuse the Project Phoenix cast?
Yes or no. If yes, the story is recast onto Jenny, David, Priya and Ben. If no,
new characters are created.
