Step 1 of 3 — Project Briefing

Client Objectives

  • Migrate the client's legacy on-premise Teradata data warehouse to Azure Databricks, adopting a modern lakehouse architecture.
  • Consolidate 14 disparate reporting systems into a single governed analytics layer.
  • Reduce month-end financial close reporting time from 9 days to 3 days.
  • Establish a foundation for future AI/ML use cases across the business.

Timeline

Weeks 1–3 Discovery & current-state assessment
Weeks 4–10 Platform build & pilot migration (Finance domain)
Weeks 11–18 Full data migration & parallel run
Weeks 19–20 Cutover & legacy decommission

⚠ Go-live is fixed to align with the client's new financial year — 20 weeks, non-negotiable.

Key Risks

High

High

Medium

Medium
